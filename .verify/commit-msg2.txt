Fix part placement inside the case and add every hardware variant

Parts were placed in an unrotated frame while the chassis was turned -90
degrees to face the glass, so every zone anchor landed up to 30cm from the
geometry it described and a seated part stood edge-on through the side panel.

- BuildScene: caseRoot now carries the single chassis turn, and zone markers,
  fasteners, cable headers, placed parts and the ghost all parent to it, so a
  case-local anchor means the same point in the mesh and the part
- pointerWorld and frameBox speak case-local too, which also fixes the camera
  fitting the wrong axis
- parts are centred on their zone anchor by measuring the mounted holder:
  buildFittedModel rests a part on y = 0, so a 44mm card used to sit 22mm high
- the carried ghost is clamped inside the chassis using the mount-aware
  extents, measured once per zone instead of per pointer move
- the ghost hides during fastener, paste, press, cable and power steps instead
  of chasing the cursor
- catalogue every model in public/models: 12 -> 35 items via
  hardwareVariants.js, with HARDWARE_CATEGORIES split out to avoid an import
  cycle; the 12 steps accept hardware by tag so variants need no plan changes
- parts bench gains a third tier and a tighter gap, keeping it 4.9m
